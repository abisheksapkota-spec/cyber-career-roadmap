Certificates and costs

  • ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC): entry-level, training and exam have been offered free through ISC2's programme. Confirm current terms on the ISC2 website before relying on it.
  • CompTIA Security+: the main target. The exam voucher is the big cost (several hundred AUD), so only book when you are scoring 80%+ on practice exams.
  • Optional extras: Microsoft SC-900 (cheap fundamentals exam), free Cisco Networking Academy courses (Networking Basics, Cybersecurity Essentials), later Network+ or CCNA.
  • Skip fee-based placement programmes. A real job or internship pays you. Spend on exam vouchers instead.
