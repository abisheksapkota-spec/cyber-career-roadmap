# Scholarships (international student from Nepal, CQU BIT, CQ18)

Checked in Oct 2026. Details change every year, so confirm with CQU before relying on anything here.

| Scholarship | Fits you? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CQUniCares Adip Rijal Memorial Scholarship | Maybe | Up to $5,000, built for international students from Nepal who show financial hardship and live and study in Queensland.
