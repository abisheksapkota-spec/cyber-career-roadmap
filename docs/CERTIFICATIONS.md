## Rules

1. Check the current exam version and price on the vendor site before booking. Security+ was on the SY0-701 exam when this was written.
2. Do not book an exam until you score 80% or more on two full practice tests in a row.
3. Spend money on exam vouchers, not on fee-based placement programmes. A real job or internship pays you.
4. Add every passed cert to LinkedIn (Licenses and Certifications) and post about it.

## Practice exam routine

- Take a full practice test, then review every wrong answer and write a one-line note on why.
- Repeat weak topics in Professor Messer videos.
- Keep a `notes/security-plus.md` file with your own summaries.
