# Daily routine

Aim for 2 to 3 hours on holiday days, less during uni term. A short session every day beats a long one once a week.

## A good day

| Time | What | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 60 min | Learn | Professor Messer video, a Cisco or TryHackMe module |
| 60 min | Practise | TryHackMe room, Sad Servers scenario, lab work |
| 15 min | Write | Notes in `notes/` or a writeup in `writeups/` |
| 5 min | Log | Add today's file in `daily-log/` (the dashboard can prefill it) |

## A low-energy day (still counts)

- 30 minutes of one thing, then log it. Keeping the streak alive matters more than a perfect day.

## Weekly review (Sunday, 15 minutes)

1. Open `CHECKLIST.md` and tick what you finished.
2. Write 3 lines in the daily log: what went well, what was hard, next week's focus.
3. Post one thing on LinkedIn if you hit a milestone.

## Streak rules

- A day counts if you add a log file for it (or mark it in the dashboard).
- Miss a day? Don't restart the plan, just log the next day.
- Update this file when your real daily routine changes. Tell Claude your routine and ask for an updated plan.
