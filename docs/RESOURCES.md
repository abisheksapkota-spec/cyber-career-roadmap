## Safety rules for your lab

- Only scan and attack machines **you own** or that a platform (TryHackMe, HackTheBox, picoCTF) says you may attack.
- Keep your lab network on **host-only** mode so vulnerable VMs never touch your real network.
- Never run exploits against real websites or people.
