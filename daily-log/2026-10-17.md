## Processes
- `ps aux`: all processes. `ps auxww`: full command lines.
- `top`: live view.
- `kill <PID>`: SIGTERM (clean), SIGKILL (force), SIGSTOP (pause).
- `systemd` is PID 1.

## Services
- `systemctl start | stop | enable | disable | status <service>`
- `enable` = start on boot.

## Background / foreground
- `&` or `Ctrl+Z` = send to background
- `fg` = bring back

## Cron
- Format: `MIN HOUR DOM MON DOW CMD`
- `crontab -l` list, `crontab -e` edit
- `@reboot` = run at boot

## Logs
- Location: `/var/log`
- Apache: `access.log` (requests), `error.log` (errors)
- Log line: `IP - - [date] "GET /file ..."`

## Troubleshooting
- Permission denied: check with `ls -l`
- No sudo: look for readable rotated logs (`access.log.1`)
- AttackBox ≠ target machine. Use SSH to reach the target.
